Honest Assessment

Key Risks

We believe in transparency. Here are the real risks every investor should understand before committing capital.

⚠️ Regulatory Risk

Cryptocurrency regulation is evolving rapidly. Different jurisdictions (EU, US, Asia) have conflicting frameworks. Token classification as a security in key markets could limit liquidity and increase compliance costs. Mitigation: legal entity in favorable jurisdiction, securities counsel on all token matters.

⚠️ Quantum Timeline Uncertainty

Q-Day might be 2030 or 2045. If quantum computers capable of breaking current cryptography arrive later than expected, urgency for PQ solutions diminishes and enterprise budget allocation may shift. Mitigation: non-PQ revenue streams (Rust performance, compliance) sustain business regardless.

⚠️ Competitive Entry

AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft have vast resources. If they build native post-quantum support into their cloud platforms within 2–3 years, our managed cloud offering faces existential competition. Mitigation: focus on the " cryptographic migration partner" niche, build deep enterprise relationships before hyperscalers respond.

⚠️ Team Execution Risk

Lévy is young. Building a cryptographic company at 14 requires exceptional mentorship and team depth. Key-person risk is real. A single senior hire (CFO, engineering lead) can dramatically de-risk the business. Mitigation: active recruitment, advisor board, and Lévy's demonstrated ability to execute.

⚠️ Token Price Volatility

Crypto markets are highly volatile. POLY token price may trade far below fundamental value during market downturns, reducing protocol revenue and governance participation. Mitigation: revenue-backed tokenomics, long vesting schedules for insiders, focus on product revenue over token speculation.

⚠️ Technical / Audit Risk

Cryptographic vulnerabilities can be catastrophic. A single implementation bug or successful side-channel attack could destroy trust in the protocol. Mitigation: third-party audits (Trail of Bits, NCC Group), formal verification, bug bounty program, conservative algorithm selection (NIST-finalized only).
